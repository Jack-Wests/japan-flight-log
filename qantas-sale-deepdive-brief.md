fed by a **Brisbane–Sydney** hop. Because we come **home from Tokyo, not Sapporo**, there are

**Option Q1 — Qantas open-jaw (ideal):** BNE→SYD→CTS **in** (arr 2–4 Feb) and Tokyo
(HND/NRT)→SYD→BNE **home** (dep 16–17 Feb), as a **multi-city** Qantas booking. Add the
**CTS→Tokyo hop on 14 Feb** (~$108pp, any carrier). Does the sale fare apply to a multi-city /
open-jaw? Often it's return-only — check.

**Option Q2 — Qantas Sapporo return + reposition for Tokyo:** BNE⇄Sapporo return (via
Sydney) on the sale, then fly **Sapporo→Tokyo→Sapporo** for the Tokyo leg so you still
depart home from Sapporo. Clunky (backtrack to Sapporo to fly home) — only worth it if the
return sale fare is *much* cheaper than the open-jaw.

**Option Q3 — Qantas one-way in + cheap one-way home:** BNE→SYD→CTS one-way on Qantas
(if the sale discounts one-ways — usually it doesn't) + cheapest Tokyo→BNE home on any carrier
+ CTS→Tokyo hop. Compare to Q1.

**Option J — Jetstar sale cross-check:** BNE→Tokyo (NRT) on the Jetstar sale, + Tokyo→CTS
+ CTS→Tokyo hops, + cheapest home. **Add a 20kg checked bag** to every Jetstar leg for Feb.

For each: **door-to-door time**, whether any layover is an overnight/airport-sleep, arrival
time into Sapporo (need ≥2 full Snow Festival days, 4–11 Feb), and whether the checked bag
