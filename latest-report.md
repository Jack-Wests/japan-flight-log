**On the Qantas Sydney–Sapporo direct (QF107):** priced fine — it runs Feb 2027 and gets you into Sapporo at 6:00pm the same day you leave (Brisbane 5:00am → Sydney → Sapporo). It's the fastest and comfiest way in, but at $1,704pp just for the flight in, it's the reason the fastest option costs ~$1,100pp more than the cheapest.

**Home leg (all options):** cheapest way home from Tokyo is via Port Moresby, landing Brisbane 9:40am — about 11h, not brutal.

**Date shifts:** none worth taking. The only shift on offer (arriving Sapporo one day late) saves just ~$66pp, under our $100 threshold, so all options stay inside the 2–5 Feb arrival / 14–18 Feb return windows.
