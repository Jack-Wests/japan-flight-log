## ⚠️ NEW RULE — Port Moresby layovers (this is the update)

The cheapest fares home often route **Tokyo → Port Moresby (POM) → Brisbane** on Air Niugini. We worked out the fare price is **not** the real price on those:

- A **short, airport-only layover** in Port Moresby (a **few hours**, staying airside, no overnight) is **fine** — treat it as a normal cheap connection.
- **Any Port Moresby routing that needs an overnight or a long stopover where you'd have to leave the airport is OFF.** Getting into and out of PNG (entry/visa faff), plus a hotel night and airport transfers, costs **more than the fare saving is worth**. So a "cheaper" long-layover Port Moresby option is really **more expensive** once those are added in.

**How to price it going forward:** if a Port Moresby option has a short airside connection, keep it. If it forces an overnight or an airport exit, **either drop it or add the real overnight cost (hotel + transfers + entry costs) before comparing** — don't show the bare fare as the winner.

*(This same logic applies to any routing with a forced overnight stopover, not just Port Moresby — a long layover you sleep through in a hotel isn't free.)*

## Flight-shape notes (for whoever's pricing)

- Cheap long layovers are fine in general — ~20h door-to-door is acceptable, up to ~30h **only if** the stopover is long enough (~8h+) to actually leave the airport and look around. Overnight airport sleeps and 3am connections are a negative — call them out.
- The cheapest total is usually built from separate legs: **Brisbane↔Tokyo international + cheap domestic hops Tokyo↔Sapporo**. Always sum every required flight (both international legs + both domestic hops) before quoting a per-person total.
- The Qantas QF107 Sydney–Sapporo seasonal direct is the comfy option but roughly double the price (~$1,787pp just one-way to get there) — it's a footnote, not a real pick.
