📋 Today's snapshot

The cheapest comfortable way to the Hokkaido snow and home again is now $1,549 per person — $6,196 for all four, or $4,647 for three if Hugh buys separately. You'd fly Singapore Airlines to Osaka and hop up to Sapporo, hop back to Tokyo after the ski, then fly home from Tokyo via Seoul: a short Parata Air flight to Seoul, then Jetstar's overnight flight to Brisbane.

That's WATCH, $49 above the $1,500 buy price. It's $25 cheaper than this afternoon's check ($1,574) and $53 down on yesterday ($1,602). Almost all of the saving is the flight home: Seoul is $55 cheaper than Singapore Airlines. The catch is that it's two separate tickets, so in Seoul you collect your bags, go through Korean border control and check in again. There's 5 hours to do it, and I've added ~$12pp for Korea's online entry permit (K-ETA), because the free pass for Australians runs out on 31 Dec 2026.
